Hermes Mail – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Hermes Mail is a personal application used only by its developer, Mohamed Lamine Meddour, with his own Google accounts.

Data accessed

With the account owner's consent, Hermes Mail accesses Gmail data (messages, labels and related metadata) through Google APIs, only to read and manage that owner's email.

Use and sharing

  • Data is used only to provide the app's features to the account owner.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Data is not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Hermes Mail's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.
